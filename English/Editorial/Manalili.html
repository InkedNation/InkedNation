The Price of an Unsafe Education

Written by Mico Wilfred L. Manalili

Schools should be safe places where students can learn, grow, and build their futures. When parents send their kids to school, they expect them to be protected within the environment, but the reality is that many schools fail to keep them safe. When a school isn't safe, learning becomes almost impossible as fear takes over confidence.

Learning becomes increasingly difficult when students are constantly confronted with fear and uncertainty. When students deal with threats like bullying, violence, or terrible school security, their mental health takes a massive hit. They end up dealing with high stress, anxiety, and depression. A student who feels unsafe is just trying to get through the day, not focusing on passing their tests.

​This fear may directly ruin academic performance. When students feel threatened in their learning environment, their academic performance may decline, which can result in absences and disengagement. Failing grades and bad attendance aren't always because a student is lazy; it can be that the school environment is so stressful that they can't focus. Education simply cannot survive in a place full of fear.

More concerning is the failure of some institutions to address security gaps and enforce protective policies before incidents escalate. They put up weak security measures or make rules about bullying that no one actually enforces. Where is the accountability? It is completely unfair for institutions to demand high grades from students when they can't even guarantee their basic safety.

​It is time for schools and leaders to take real responsibility. School safety isn't just an option—it is a necessity. If we don't fix these security gaps and protect our students today, we are failing the future of our youth.
