Background


Inked Nation (Tintang Bayan) is a student journalistic publication dedicated to raising awareness of social issues that affect individuals and communities, with a particular focus on school insecurity and its psychological impact on students. It seeks to explore the realities behind violence, fear, and the challenges of maintaining safe learning environments. It aims to inform the public, amplify voices, encourage critical discussions, and promote accountability in addressing issues that threaten students' well-being and right to education.

Inked Nation : Represents a nation shaped by words, where writing and journalism serve as powerful tools to inform, expose issues, and give people a voice.

Tintang Bayan : Sumasagisag sa tinig ng mamamayan sa pamamagitan ng panulat. Layunin nitong ilantad ang mga suliranin sa lipunan, magpalaganap ng kamalayan, at itaguyod ang pananagutan sa pamamagitan ng pamamahayag.
