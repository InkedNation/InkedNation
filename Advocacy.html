Advocacy Video

"As One"


As One shows the overlooked effects of school violence, bullying, and social isolation on students, especially in recent times of school violence. It portrays how some students experience loneliness while others find comfort and belonging through building new friendships; it also shows empathy and standing together against obstacles. We encourage people to recognize that even those who appear quiet or unnoticed may be facing struggles that are not visible to all people. No one should have to face their struggles alone, and a simple act of kindness, understanding, and support can make a meaningful difference to someone’s life.
